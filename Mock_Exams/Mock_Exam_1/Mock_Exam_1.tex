\begin{task} [20 points -- 10 subtasks worth 2 points each]
An online shop for sports equipment plans with a mean order value of
$\mu_0 = 80$~EUR. From many years of experience, the standard deviation of the order
values is known: $\sigma = 12$~EUR. After a redesign of the website, a random sample of
$n = 36$ orders gives a mean order value of $\bar x = 84.30$~EUR.
The significance level is $\alpha = 0.05$. Because $\sigma$ is known, the test and the
confidence interval are based on the standard normal distribution (Table~1).

\medskip
\noindent\textit{All subtasks can be solved independently of each other.}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\alph*)},leftmargin=1.9em,itemsep=10pt,topsep=4pt]
\keepitem{5}\item The management wants to find out whether the mean order value after the redesign \emph{differs} from the planning figure of $80$~EUR (in either direction). State the hypothesis pair. \pts
\keepitem{5}\item Compute the standard error $\sigma/\sqrt{n}$ of the sample mean for $\sigma = 12$~EUR and $n = 36$. \pts
\keepitem{5}\item Compute the test statistic $Z_n$ for $\bar x = 84.30$~EUR, $\mu_0 = 80$~EUR, $\sigma = 12$~EUR and $n = 36$. \pts
\keepitem{5}\item In Python, the critical value of the two-sided test at the significance level $\alpha = 0.05$ ($\sigma$ known) is computed with the line below. Which number must be passed as \texttt{q}?\\*[3pt]\hspace*{1.5em}\texttt{\upshape stats.norm.ppf(q)} \pts
\keepitem{5}\item The test statistic is $Z_n = 2.15$ and the critical value is $z_{0.975} = 1.96$. Make the test decision for $H_0\colon \mu = 80$ vs.\ $H_1\colon \mu \neq 80$ at $\alpha = 0.05$. \pts
\keepitem{5}\item Compute the two-sided $p$-value for the test statistic $Z_n = 2.15$ (use Table~1). \pts
\keepitem{5}\item Compute the $95\,\%$ confidence interval for $\mu$ with $\bar x = 84.30$~EUR, $\sigma/\sqrt{n} = 2$~EUR and $z_{0.975} = 1.96$. \pts
\keepitem{5}\item For the mean order value $\mu$, the $95\,\%$ confidence interval $[80.38;\ 88.22]$~EUR was computed. Interpret this interval in one sentence. \pts
\keepitem{5}\item Describe in one sentence what a Type~I error would mean in this situation. \pts
\keepitem{5}\item In practice $\sigma$ is often unknown. Suppose the 36 order values are stored in a NumPy array \texttt{orders} and $\sigma$ is estimated from the data. Give the \texttt{scipy.stats} command that carries out the two-sided one-sample $t$-test of $H_0\colon \mu = 80$. \pts
\end{enumerate}
\end{task}

\begin{task} [20 points -- 10 subtasks worth 2 points each]
\textbf{Part A (subtasks a--h).} A caf\'e records on five summer days the daily
maximum temperature $x$ (in $^{\circ}$C) and the number $y$ of iced coffees sold:

\medskip
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lccccc}
\toprule
Day $i$ & 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 & 5\\
\midrule
Maximum temperature $x_i$ ($^{\circ}$C) & 20 & 22 & 24 & 26 & 28\\
Iced coffees sold $y_i$ & 46 & 54 & 62 & 70 & 68\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\medskip
\noindent Model: $y_i = \beta_0 + \beta_1 x_i + u_i$. Auxiliary values:
\begin{gather*}
\bar x = 24, \qquad \bar y = 60, \qquad S_{xx} = \sum (x_i-\bar x)^2 = 40,\\
S_{xy} = \sum (x_i-\bar x)(y_i-\bar y) = 120, \qquad S_{yy} = \sum (y_i-\bar y)^2 = 400.
\end{gather*}

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Part B (subtasks i--j).} For a larger sample of $n = 60$ summer days the
caf\'e also records the rainfall \texttt{rain} (in mm) and estimates a multiple linear
regression of \texttt{sales} (iced coffees sold) on \texttt{temp} (maximum temperature in
$^{\circ}$C) and \texttt{rain}. Excerpt of the \texttt{statsmodels} output:
\begin{footnotesize}
\begin{verbatim}

Model:                            OLS   Adj. R-squared:                  0.945
Method:                 Least Squares   F-statistic:                     512.4
                 coef    std err          t      P>|t|      [0.025      0.975]
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Intercept    -12.2152      3.783     -3.229      0.002     -19.790      -4.641
temp           3.0055      0.131     22.863      0.000       2.742       3.269
rain          -0.4313      0.374     -1.154      0.253      -1.180       0.317
\end{verbatim}
\end{footnotesize}

\noindent\textit{All subtasks can be solved independently of each other.}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\alph*)},leftmargin=1.9em,itemsep=10pt,topsep=4pt]
\keepitem{5}\item Compute the slope $\hat\beta_1$ from $S_{xy} = 120$ and $S_{xx} = 40$. \pts
\keepitem{5}\item Compute the intercept $\hat\beta_0$ from $\hat\beta_1 = 3$, $\bar x = 24$ and $\bar y = 60$. \pts
\keepitem{5}\item Interpret the slope $\hat\beta_1 = 3$ in one sentence in the context of the caf\'e. \pts
\keepitem{5}\item The fitted regression of \texttt{sales} on \texttt{temp} is stored in the Python object \texttt{reg}; its estimated line is $\hat y = -12 + 3x$. Which value does the following line return?\\*[3pt]\hspace*{1.5em}\texttt{\upshape reg.predict(pd.DataFrame(\{"temp":\ [25]\}))} \pts
\keepitem{5}\item Compute the residual $\hat u_5$ of day~5 with $(x_5;\ y_5) = (28;\ 68)$, using the estimated regression line $\hat y = -12 + 3x$. \pts
\keepitem{5}\item For the five days, the total sum of squares is $\text{TSS} = 400$ and the residual sum of squares is $\text{RSS} = 40$. Compute the coefficient of determination $R^2$. \pts
\keepitem{5}\item Determine the correlation coefficient $r_{xy}$ from $R^2 = 0.9$ and the slope $\hat\beta_1 = 3$. \pts
\keepitem{5}\item The five days are stored in a pandas DataFrame \texttt{cafe} with the columns \texttt{temp} and \texttt{sales}. Give the \texttt{statsmodels} command that estimates the simple linear regression of \texttt{sales} on \texttt{temp}. \pts
\keepitem{5}\item In the multiple regression of Part~B, the coefficient of \texttt{temp} is $\hat\beta_1 = 3.0055$. Interpret this coefficient in one sentence. \pts
\keepitem{5}\item In the multiple regression of Part~B, the coefficient of \texttt{rain} is $-0.4313$ with the $p$-value $0.253$ (column \texttt{P>|t|}). Decide at $\alpha = 0.05$ whether rainfall has a significant effect on sales when the temperature is held fixed. \pts
\end{enumerate}
\end{task}

\begin{task} [20 points -- 10 subtasks worth 2 points each]
A mobile-phone provider wants to predict whether a customer cancels the contract
in the next quarter ($Y = 1$: churn, $Y = 0$: no churn). The explanatory variable $x$ is the
number of calls to the service hotline in the last six months. A logistic regression gives
the estimated linear predictor (log-odds)
\[ \hat\eta(x) = \hat\beta_0 + \hat\beta_1 x = -3 + 0.5\,x
   \qquad\text{and}\qquad \hat p(x) = \frac{e^{\hat\eta(x)}}{1+e^{\hat\eta(x)}} . \]
The provider then classifies $n = 200$ test customers with the threshold $t = 0.5$
(predicted churn if $\hat p(x) > 0.5$):

\medskip
\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.3}
\begin{tabular}{l|cc|c}
& Pred.\ No & Pred.\ Yes & Total\\
\hline
Actual No & $\mathrm{TN} = 140$ & $\mathrm{FP} = 20$ & $160$\\
Actual Yes & $\mathrm{FN} = 10$ & $\mathrm{TP} = 30$ & $40$\\
\hline
Total & $150$ & $50$ & $200$\\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\medskip
\noindent\textit{All subtasks can be solved independently of each other.}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\alph*)},leftmargin=1.9em,itemsep=10pt,topsep=4pt]
\keepitem{5}\item Last year, $20\,\%$ of all customers churned, i.e.\ $p = 0.2$. Compute the odds of churn. \pts
\keepitem{5}\item Compute the predicted churn probability $\hat p(x)$ of a customer with $x = 2$ hotline calls, using $\hat\eta(x) = -3 + 0.5\,x$ and $e^{-2} = 0.1353$. \pts
\keepitem{5}\item The odds ratio of the hotline calls is $e^{\hat\beta_1} = e^{0.5} = 1.6487$. Interpret it in one sentence. \pts
\keepitem{5}\item A customer has $x = 7$ hotline calls. Classify this customer with the threshold $t = 0.5$, using $\hat\eta(x) = -3 + 0.5\,x$ (you may use $e^{0.5} = 1.6487$). \pts
\keepitem{5}\item The customer data are stored in a pandas DataFrame \texttt{df} with the columns \texttt{churn} (coded $0/1$) and \texttt{calls}. Give the \texttt{statsmodels} command that estimates the logistic regression of \texttt{churn} on \texttt{calls}. \pts
\keepitem{5}\item Compute the accuracy of the classifier from the confusion matrix with $\mathrm{TN} = 140$, $\mathrm{FP} = 20$, $\mathrm{FN} = 10$ and $\mathrm{TP} = 30$. \pts
\keepitem{5}\item The DataFrame \texttt{test} holds the actual class \texttt{churn} (coded $0/1$) of the test customers, \texttt{phat} their predicted churn probabilities; at $t = 0.5$ the confusion matrix has $\mathrm{TN} = 140$, $\mathrm{FP} = 20$, $\mathrm{FN} = 10$ and $\mathrm{TP} = 30$. Which value does the following line show in the row \texttt{churn}~$= 1$, column \texttt{True}?\\*[3pt]\hspace*{1.5em}\texttt{\upshape pd.crosstab(test.churn,\ phat > 0.5,\ normalize="index")} \pts
\keepitem{5}\item Compute the specificity of the classifier from the confusion matrix with $\mathrm{TN} = 140$, $\mathrm{FP} = 20$, $\mathrm{FN} = 10$ and $\mathrm{TP} = 30$. \pts
\keepitem{5}\item Compute the precision of the classifier from the confusion matrix with $\mathrm{TN} = 140$, $\mathrm{FP} = 20$, $\mathrm{FN} = 10$ and $\mathrm{TP} = 30$. \pts
\keepitem{5}\item To catch more churners, the provider lowers the threshold from $t = 0.5$ to $t = 0.3$. Does the sensitivity increase or decrease? \pts
\end{enumerate}
\end{task}
